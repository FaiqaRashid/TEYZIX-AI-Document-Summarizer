Artificial Intelligence, or AI, is transforming the modern digital world at an incredible pace.
At its core, artificial intelligence refers to the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines and computer systems.
Machine learning is a critical subset of artificial intelligence that focuses on building systems that learn from data.
Instead of being explicitly programmed with strict rules, machine learning algorithms use statistical techniques to identify patterns in massive datasets.
Deep learning takes this concept even further by utilizing artificial neural networks inspired by the human brain.
These complex multi-layered neural networks are exceptionally good at processing unstructured data like raw images, video streams, and audio files.
However, the rapid growth of artificial intelligence also introduces significant ethical challenges.
